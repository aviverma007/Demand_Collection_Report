## 🎯 **Each Chart Explained**

### **1. Top Milestones by Demand**
Shows the 8 highest-demand milestones with both demand (blue) and collected (green) amounts. Helps identify which milestones are most valuable.

### **2. Tower-wise Performance**

Line chart showing collection rates for all milestones. Purple line trending upward is good.

### **5. Demand vs Collection**
Area chart comparing total demand (blue area) vs total collected (green area). Shows the gap clearly.

### **6. Tower Efficiency**
Radar chart comparing collection rate performance of all 6 towers. Helps identify which towers collect best.
